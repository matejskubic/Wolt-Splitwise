Forgot to open the checkout page? Read discounts from a screenshot with AI

Per-person discounts are only shown on the group order checkout page (with the Ready tab selected). The order history page only shows full prices and one total discount. If you have a screenshot of the checkout page, an AI assistant can turn it into per-person amounts:

  1. Take a screenshot of the Participants list on the checkout page (e.g. Win+Shift+S).
  2. Open an AI chat that accepts images (Copilot, ChatGPT, …), paste the screenshot and the prompt below.
  3. Copy the JSON answer, open the order on wolt.com → Profile → Order history, click the extension and paste it under Paste split amounts.

Each amount is what the participant pays after item discounts and before service/delivery fees – the highlighted price, not the struck-through one (e.g. €8.67, not 12.39). Fees are taken from the order history page and split across everyone. CSV such as Matej Skubic,8.67 (one person per line) works too.
